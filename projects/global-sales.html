Global Sales and Supply Chain Analytics

An end-to-end analytics solution designed to transform global sales and supply chain data into actionable business insights.

The project combines Excel, Python, Pandas, SQL, Snowflake and Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, market performance, delivery efficiency and supply chain operations.

180,519 Records
1 Dataset

$3.97M Total Profit

Project Overview

The global sales dataset contains 180,519 transaction records. The project follows a complete analytics workflow from raw data preparation and transformation to SQL analysis, cloud data warehousing and interactive Power BI dashboards.

Raw Data → Excel → Python / Pandas → Snowflake → SQL Analysis → Power BI → Business Insights

Analytics Areas

Sales Performance

Analyzed overall sales performance, revenue trends, product performance and business growth patterns.

Profitability

Evaluated total profit, profit margins and profitability across products, customers and markets.

Customer & Market

Identified customer-level and market-level performance drivers and business opportunities.

Delivery & Supply Chain

Examined shipping time, delivery delays, on-time performance and supply chain efficiency.
