Healthcare Analytics Intelligence

An end-to-end healthcare analytics solution designed to transform patient, hospital, admission, diagnosis and billing data into actionable business and clinical insights.

The project combines Python, SQL, MySQL, Snowflake, Power BI and DAX to analyze patient populations, hospital performance, operations, financial outcomes and clinical activity.

Project Overview

The healthcare dataset contains 597,774 records across patients, admissions, billing, diagnoses and hospitals. The project follows a complete analytics workflow from raw data preparation to business intelligence dashboards.
